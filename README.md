## 🕹️ About

Citystate Metropolis is a single-player city-building and management simulation set around designing, governing, and expanding a living metropolis. Shape neighborhoods with grid-less zoning, procedural buildings, custom roads, terrain tools, budgets, economies, and traffic systems. This unofficial leaked pre-release build surfaced ahead of the planned 2026 Early Access launch and is not affiliated with the developer or publisher.

For city-builder players who want deep planning tools, realistic growth systems, and an unfinished build with rough edges still showing.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏙️ **Gridless City Design** — Lay out districts and neighborhoods without being locked to a traditional grid.
- 🏗️ **Procedural Buildings** — Generate residential, commercial, industrial, service, and utility buildings.
- 🛣️ **Spline-Based Roads** — Build flexible road networks with dozens of road styles.
- 🎨 **Building Editor** — Customize building footprints, setbacks, floors, colors, shapes, and parking.
- 💰 **Urban Economy** — Balance budgets, collect taxes, manage growth, and shape land values.
- 🚦 **Traffic Simulation** — Plan infrastructure around a functioning city-wide traffic system.
- 🌍 **World Builder** — Create terrains with procedural generation, real-world data, forests, slopes, rivers, and lakes.
- 📊 **Socioeconomic Simulation** — Watch policies and building regulations influence income disparity, gentrification, and urban decay.
